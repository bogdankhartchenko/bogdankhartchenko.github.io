Rules first, drawings second

Tone accuracy

Brand recognition comes from editorial intelligence, not visual uniformity. A satirical piece and a regulatory analysis shouldn’t look alike. The logo and type stay fixed; palette and framing are guidelines, not hard rules. Sameness is just recognizable mediocrity

The taxonomy

At volume, the real design system is the content taxonomy. I built them in the first four weeks from the stories we were already publishing: grouped by what the art had to show, they fell into 14 recurring types. Those 14 categories decided each story’s visual treatment before a single stroke. Get the category right and the illustration follows

Speed

The 25-minute target came before any design work, and it came from capacity: 8–10 stories at 25 minutes is 3–4 hours of drawing a day, which two people could carry next to briefs, approvals and posting. Strong concepts that took 40 minutes got cut. Speed was a design decision, not a compromise
