Research

Research

The audience

71%

of B2B buyers who see personal value in a supplier buy from it. Without it, 23%

→ A name that sounds like an ally, open to a small business and fit for a corporate one

Google and CEB, From Promotion to Emotion, 2013, a survey of 3,000 B2B buyers. BRO sells B2B and B2C at once, from Nova Poshta, Carlsberg and Miele to a single clinic, a school and a restaurant

The name

12/21

competitors name themselves after the category. None uses an everyday word

→ No descriptive name. BRO is an everyday word in English and Ukrainian

Audit of 21 Ukrainian furniture makers, 2023. No competitor has a three-letter, one-syllable real word. Real words are recalled better than invented ones (Lerman and Garbarino, 2002), and a name that describes one benefit makes later, unrelated claims harder to remember (Keller, Heckler and Houston, 1998)

The logo

10/21

competitor logos add a symbol. At least 4 draw a chair or a sofa, and every one is a static mark

→ No furniture in the logo. Letters, pattern and generator share two blocks

Audit of 21 Ukrainian furniture makers, 2023. In a sofa-ad experiment, a round logo made the product seem more comfortable and an angular one more durable (Jiang, Gorn, Galli and Chattopadhyay, 2016). BRO’s letters use both

The colour

17/21

competitors use an accent colour, most often red. Only 4 are black and white

→ Keep the client’s mono palette and put the difference into shape

Audit of 21 Ukrainian furniture makers, 2023. Research on distinctive brand assets ranks colour as the asset least likely to be both unique and famous, and logos and fonts as the most likely (Romaniuk, 2018)
